# Changelog

## 1.5.0
- New: **Smooth Twist** spreads a strand's twist evenly from root to tip without changing cross-section shapes.

## 1.4.0
- New: **Follow Rings** option, so Create Shaper Curve works on strands that are already bent.

## 1.3.0
- New: **Untwist** option in Tidy Strand.

## 1.2.0
- New: **Tidy Strand** makes cross-sections round and evenly spaced and smooths the strand.

## 1.1.0
- **Whole Strand** and **Single Row** modes, **Cancel Shaping**, Traditional Chinese interface.

## 1.0.0
- First version: live Bezier curve shaping of a single row of vertices.
